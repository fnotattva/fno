# FnO Tattva NSE EOD Futures + Options OI Radar

**Research-only, ₹0, NSE official end-of-day data, no broker/API login, no intraday monitor, no real orders.**

## Validated inputs
- NSE F&O UDiFF Common Bhavcopy Final: `BhavCopy_NSE_FO_0_0_0_YYYYMMDD_F_0000.csv.zip`.
- NSE CM UDiFF Common Bhavcopy: `BhavCopy_NSE_CM_0_0_0_YYYYMMDD_F_0000.csv.zip`.
- Workflow uses a ZIP-capable GitHub-hosted Python runner, so a chat web-reader's inability to open `application/zip` is irrelevant.
- Validates each ZIP, CSV field schema, trade date, existence of stock futures (`STF`) and stock options (`STO`), same underlying/expiry coverage, historical CM OHLCV consistency.
- Runner and tests use only Python standard library and GitHub Actions' free public-repository runners.

## Calculations
- Select the nearest non-expired `STF` contract for each stock; ignore expiry/roll window (<=3 calendar days).
- Classify futures settlement movement vs previous settlement and same-contract daily OI movement into long buildup / short buildup / short covering / long unwinding. This is *not* proof of which side initiated the trading.
- Use a complete available `STO` strike list for the same underlying and futures expiry. Identify highest-PE-OI put wall, highest-CE-OI call wall, relevant nearby walls, PCR and max pain (minimum intrinsic settlement payout across all listed strikes).
- Calculate OI changes directly from exchange `ChngInOpnIntrst`, with aggregate put/call OI change for context; not proof of option writing or unwinding transactions.
- Require at least 65 valid chronological CM sessions (the daily downloader requests up to 115 calendar days) to calculate **EMA20, EMA50, SMA20, SMA50, RSI14 (Wilder), MACD 12/26/9, recent EMA/SMA/MACD crosses, relative volume and ATR14**.
- Compute Fibonacci levels (23.6%, 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%, 78.6%) from the previous **60 completed session** high/low, deliberately excluding today's candle to avoid lookahead. These are contextual support/resistance observations, not automatic reversal or target predictions.
- Mandatory technical confirmation: cash close and EMA20/SMA20 on the correct side of the trend, EMA20 vs EMA50 and SMA20 vs SMA50 alignment, rising/falling EMA20, RSI14 52–70 for longs or 30–48 for shorts, directional MACD signal/histogram, relative cash volume >=1.20x. Recent trend cross and five-session breakout strengthen the score. Reject overextended RSI or a contradictory signal even if futures OI is strong.
- Only **fresh futures LONG BUILDUP** qualifies for long recommendations and **fresh futures SHORT BUILDUP** for short recommendations, with >=2% same-contract OI change and >=0.5% futures daily price move. Short covering and long unwinding are diagnostics, **not standalone recommendations**.
- Independent score gates: futures >=35/40, options >=23/35, technicals >=23/25, aggregate >=85/100. Scores represent rule confluence and have NOT been calibrated to winning probabilities. A zero-pick day is valid and should simply say **NO TRADE**; never force a daily recommendation.
- Only produce conditional momentum WATCH research signals when fresh futures buildup, option-chain context, technical confluence, liquidity, structural levels, and risk rules pass together.
- Cash stock price defines entry/SL/targets. **Do not place a stock-futures trade at a cash-equity level**; futures trade levels require recalculation for basis and tick size.
- Initial SL uses day's range plus ATR buffer; T1, T2 and stretch T3 ~2.55R / 3.55R / 4.55R; modelled 0.15% of price round-trip cost is a scenario assumption, not actual charges. Net modelled T1 R:R must be >=2; risk <=4.5% and target distance <=11% of entry. Gap risk and slippage may make actual risk much higher.
- Intraday confirmation is not available from EOD files. Entry is contingent on next-session five-minute price confirmation *checked by the user/broker*, which the job cannot verify. No automatic executions or exit alerts.

## Three separate research outputs — no quota
**A. Strict high-conviction research opportunities** are unchanged. Any number may qualify, including NONE, requiring the existing futures/option/technical score gates, usable risk and 2R+ net modelled targets. NEVER downgrade a failed strict candidate to an A-tier trade just to fill slots.

**B. Expert Conditional Trade Plans — level-based entries valid for 1–2 subsequent sessions** are a separate, *lower-conviction* research category (zero or more may qualify). They come from an independent EOD price/volume/futures-OI pattern, **not** from downgrading rejected strict A-tier signals. They require a valid stop loss no farther than 4.5% from the proposed cash entry, a verified same-expiry option chain, minimum liquidity and 1.35× relative volume, compatible futures and EMA20/MACD momentum, and an **independent resistance/support or opposing OI wall for T1**. T1 is slightly *before* the nearest confirmed structural barrier, not arbitrarily projected to 2R. Unlike A-tier, a B-tier plan may have modelled net T1 R:R below 2, but not below 0.75; such a plan is **not proven profitable** and may be unsuitable given fees, slippage and hit-rate. No reliable structural T1, unsafe stop or contradictory evidence => no B-tier plan. T2/T3 use farther independent historical levels when available; otherwise they are explicitly marked ATR/risk **projections**, not predicted or verified barriers. Report cash entry, stop, T1/T2/T3, the source of each target, net modelled R multiples, initial risk %, supporting/contrary indicators, and a gap/chase filter. Plans require a next-session 5-minute close across entry with supportive volume, which EOD data cannot confirm. Plan validity expires after the next **two** observed NSE trading sessions, with EOD-only level observations and no fabricated fills. No daily quota and no intraday execution or position monitoring.

**C. Expert Opportunities — two paths: lower-conviction conditional plans (exceptional additional cases allowed) OR WATCH ONLY (up to 3 non-actionable highlights)**. Only a C plan with a verified current-session cash + futures + same-expiry option chain + sufficient cash history, a meaningful structure pattern, independent technical support, a safe invalidation at <=4.5% cash price risk, and nearby support/resistance T1 may be issued. Do not simply assign attractive R multiples to a watch. Lower-conviction C-level plans can tolerate some nonconforming A/B technical checks when at least two current evidence channels agree, but never a verified opposite directional futures buildup. A cash close too far from prior 20-session breakout structure normally disqualifies a wide-breakout candle. When a strong 20-session breakout has no sensible close-of-day SL and the previous breakout level is within 1.5 ATR of the close, an explicitly labelled **pullback/retest** candidate may be assessed with entry near prior 20-session high/low and SL beyond that same breakout structure; it must actually retrace and show a fresh five-minute reclaim/rejection with volume before entry. Any C plan must provide entry, SL, T1/T2/T3 with per-target modelled net R multiples and source of each target. Net T1 >=0.60R is a *floor*, not a desired fixed target; the closest independently established structural obstacle determines T1. Show gross price references at 0.6R, 1.5R and 3R only as separate benchmarks, and flag T2/T3 when projections rather than grounded market levels. At a 0.6R full exit, a win rate greater than 62.5% is required to break even *before costs*, so this category is emphatically **unvalidated as a profitable strategy**.

The residual independent C WATCH ONLY list retains the original discretionary pattern logic for ideas lacking safe stop, independent target or sufficient chain confirmation. are a separate, optional list of up to 3 other stocks that could merit professional review despite failing *any number* of strict technical, OI or options filters. The engine explores independently observable NSE cash-price/volume breakouts or breakdowns, futures OI expansion near inflection levels, and reversal/rejection near relevant option walls. It can consider a stock even if its options chain is incomplete (disclosed). Each idea requires verifiable same-day NSE cash+futures EOD inputs, minimum cash turnover ~₹15 crore, futures OI/volume, historical-price coverage, a nontrivial volume and price-structure pattern, and a clear next-session decision level. Watch items disclose **why interesting**, **which strict filters remain unconfirmed/conflict**, **nearby options walls**, **price level requiring further confirmation**, and **whether an indicative stop is even sensible**. If no acceptable stop exists, explicitly state NOT TRADEABLE YET; do not invent SL/targets.

The secondary list is algorithmically generated and audited for patterns; it is **not equivalent to independent manual human/AI review of all company news**. An additional ChatGPT review may vet available verified corporate disclosures, market conditions and contradictory data before presentation, but must not invent catalysts or claim unseen events. Neither list is required to contain any stocks. A strict A-tier NO TRADE day may still have 0–3 conditional B-tier plans, 0–3 additional guarded C-tier conditional plans, and/or 0–3 C-tier WATCH ONLY ideas. B and C never inherit the A-tier label; C-tier watches without verified entry/stop/reward evidence never carry invented trade levels. If nothing credible meets even the secondary evidence requirements, state no additional credible ideas.

## Schedule and delivery
- The runner uses the official 2026 NSE cash/F&O holiday calendar. A listed holiday (including 10 Nov 2026, Diwali-Balipratipada) produces **MARKET HOLIDAY — NO EOD SCAN REQUIRED**, without attempting downloads, reusing stale quotes or issuing A/B picks. Historical downloads also skip exchange holidays. Update the calendar for future years; special Muhurat sessions are not treated as regular weekday EOD sessions.
- GitHub Actions workflow `.github/workflows/nse-eod-research.yml` attempts the EOD analysis at **19:15, 19:55, and 20:55 IST** (13:45/14:25/15:25 UTC) weekdays. These are short fallback checks for late official publication or delayed scheduling, not continuous market-hour scanning. GitHub cron may delay/drop an event; the daily ChatGPT report is an independent **exact-time 21:15 IST** weekday task. It must retrieve and validate the current market date, or clearly state NO VERIFIED TRADE.
- If the latest day's FO or CM archive is inaccessible, the job writes `NO VERIFIED TRADE` instead of silently issuing a pick from a prior day. On a valid session when no candidate passes every filter, it writes **NO TRADE**, not a forced recommendation.
- Workflow creates **Actions artifacts** `nse-eod-research-report` containing `report.md` and `report.json`, retained for 14 days. This workflow does not publish picks to the application's code or commit them to the public repository.
- Stores limited A/B/C proposal and EOD-scenario state through GitHub Actions cache (best effort; cache may expire). It never knows which actual positions were traded, booked, stopped, or partially sold. If cache is not restored, the daily bulletin must disclose missing continuity, not pretend open positions have been verified.
- Separate ChatGPT EOD notification should run **after** this workflow and retrieve the latest successful report artifact using the connected GitHub tool. The GitHub Actions service and the ChatGPT scheduled task are separate processes. A passing download is not proof that the ChatGPT task retrieved the report.

## Owner refinement — daily risk management, 12 November 2026
- An **entry proposal is valid for only 2 subsequent regular NSE sessions**. No EOD price-level touch within those sessions => cancel/expire. An EOD touch is *not* proof of a 5-minute confirming candle or a fill.

- Propose a **NEXT-session** protective stop using previous five-session price structure, EMA20 and ATR, subject to a monotonic no-loosening rule. When an EOD level touched T1, propose at least breakeven-plus-cost protection conditional on a verified position, but never claim that it was automatically placed or that today was protected by the newly proposed level.
